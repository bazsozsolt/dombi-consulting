The book

The Practical AI Desk

A practical handbook for the years ahead

A handbook for people who already have work to do and want a lasting habit with AI assistants. It sets out a simple operating rule — brief, review, own — and a workspace you can come back to when the product names change. It is not a promise of a new career, a catalogue of secret prompts, or a claim that a subscription is a business.

The book includes chapter-end checklists and a 12-sheet prompt workbook. It is available on Amazon as a Kindle edition and a paperback. Published 17 November 2026. ISBN 9798142703019.
